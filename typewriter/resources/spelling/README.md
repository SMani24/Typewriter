# English spelling wordlist

`english.txt.gz` combines the `wamerican` and `wbritish` wordlists from the local
Debian/Ubuntu packages, version 2020.12.07-2. These are SCOWL-derived spelling lists.
Lines were case-folded, deduplicated, sorted, encoded as UTF-8, and compressed with
a fixed gzip timestamp. Both British and American spellings are accepted.

The complete distribution notice from `/usr/share/doc/wamerican/copyright` is
preserved in [COPYRIGHT](COPYRIGHT), including the original sources and permission
notices. No external service is needed to check an essay.
