How are my review words chosen?

Due words come first. A short review uses words whose next review time has arrived and which have material for an enabled spelling exercise. Every fifth normal spelling question uses the oldest due eligible word. This credit carries across short sessions; selected and endless reviews do not use it.

Recent struggles guide Legacy priority. We use the last eight spaced spelling recalls: the first due, unaided answer per word per UTC day since this upgrade. More mistakes raise priority, and the latest miss or revealed spelling adds a boost. Waiting overdue raises priority gradually. New words start with a neutral score. Equal scores use oldest due time.

Fixed stages space your reviews. A due unaided success can advance a word once per UTC day, with intervals of 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days. Extra successes keep its date and stage unchanged. Misses or hints reduce the stage and bring it back in ten minutes. Drills and correction repetitions do not advance stages.

Fresh cards come first. Due words without saved FSRS memory appear first, oldest due first. Other due words are ordered by their lowest estimated chance of recall at the current time, with due time breaking ties. Older answers remain in history; they are not replayed into fresh FSRS cards.

Dates adapt to due answers. A due correct, unaided spelling answer is rated Good; a due miss or revealed spelling is rated Again. FSRS uses short learning or relearning steps before longer adaptive intervals, so a word can be due several times in one day. It targets 90% retention; this is a scheduling target, not a guarantee.

Extra practice stays separate. Early answers, drills, immediate corrections and meaning quizzes do not train FSRS spelling memory. The daily spaced-recall statistic counts the first due unaided answer per UTC day, while FSRS can learn from several genuinely due steps that day.

Meanings have their own ranking. Meaning quizzes use their own recent results. Endless review gives due spelling words priority, then revisits other eligible words when none are due. Selected reviews practise your selection, even before it is due. Lifetime answer totals retain older history and extra practice.
